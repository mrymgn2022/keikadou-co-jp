Drink, nibble, finish with soba.

Four late-night sakaba under Yushima Tenjin shrine, Tokyo

Welcome to the KADOYA GroupYushima, Bunkyo, Tokyo

We run four small drinking places within two minutes' walk of each other, just below Yushima Tenjin shrine and a few minutes from Ueno. It started with a bar on a street corner ("kado" means corner). Today there is also an izakaya serving food and sake from the owner's home region in northern Miyagi, and two "soba sakaba", where you drink first and finish with a bowl of buckwheat noodles. All four open at 6 pm and stay open until the small hours. All are closed on Sundays.

The copper-mug highballour signature

Suntory Kakubin, Japan's everyday whisky since 1937, topped with soda in a copper mug straight from the freezer. The metal keeps the drink ice-cold to the last sip and the thin rim changes the way it tastes. ¥500 at Bar Kadoya, and on the list at all four shops.

Not a whisky drinker? There are "Akadama" sweet-wine highballs, draught Premium Malt's, shochu, cocktails and Miyagi sake at Kuriharaya.

Good to knowhow a sakaba works

  • Otoshi. A small starter is served automatically and charged (a few hundred yen). At Kuriharaya it is a little vegetable hot pot.
  • Ordering. Order a drink first, then food as you go. Menus are in Japanese; staff can point things out, and photos on this site help.
  • Solo drinkers are welcome at the counter at all four shops. Groups of up to about six fit at tables; larger parties should phone ahead.
  • Paying. Pay at the end, cards accepted. No tipping.
  • Smoking. Please ask staff about the current policy at each shop.
